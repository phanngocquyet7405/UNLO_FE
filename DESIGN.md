# UNLO — Design System (Celestial Indigo, Periwinkle & Milky Way Ivory)

Designed around the UNLO Moodboard, Official UNLO Logo, and Zazzle-inspired custom cards, wedding invitations & personalized gifts experience.

## 1. Visual World & Moodboard Palette
- **Midnight Indigo (`--foreground` / `--unlo-midnight`)**: `#15095D` — primary ink, logo outer stroke, and App Icon surface.
- **Royal Indigo (`--primary` / `--unlo-iris-deep`)**: `#32259B` & `#443EB1` — primary interactive CTAs and UNLO logo fill.
- **Periwinkle & Lilac (`--unlo-lilac` / `--unlo-mist`)**: `#8D8DC7`, `#9EA1D2`, `#BBC2DD` — hero pill CTA, Sale highlight circle, active accents.
- **Meteor Ice Canvas (`--secondary` / `--unlo-meteor`)**: `#EFF2F9` — clean cool product card backdrops and alternating studio bands.
- **Milky Way Ivory (`--unlo-ivory`)**: `#FCFDF1` — warm editorial contrast surfaces, paper card previews, and trust strips.
- **Galaxy & Deep Sea (`--unlo-galaxy` / `--unlo-deep-sea`)**: `#0B2056`, `#183563`, `#2B467C`, `#334EAB`, `#6B94D6` — hero editorial backdrop and newsletter band.
- **Cloud Mist (`--border`)**: `#DCE0E7` — hairline structural borders.

## 2. Typography
- **Editorial Headings & Small Editorial Copy (`--font-serif`)**: `Libre Bodoni` (Google Fonts, Vietnamese subset, weights 400–700 regular & italic).
- **English Display Accents (`--font-display`)**: `Climate Crisis` (Google Fonts, weight 400) used selectively for bold English editorial stamps (`HOLIDAY & WEDDING STUDIO`, `BEST SELLERS`, `CREATE YOUR OWN`, `UNLO STUDIO CLUB`).
- **UI Controls & Metadata (`--font-sans`)**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Vietnamese subset) with `tabular-nums` for prices and counters.

## 3. Storefront Content Architecture (Zazzle-Inspired)
- **Categories**: Thiệp Giáng Sinh (`christmas-cards`), Thiệp Cưới (`wedding-invitations`), Thiệp & Sự Kiện (`invitations`), Quà Cá Nhân Hóa (`personalized-gifts`), Cốc & Decor (`home-living`), Danh Thiếp & In Ấn (`stationery`).
- **Signature Feature**: Live interactive **UNLO Customizer / Create Your Own** preview on both the homepage and product detail pages (custom headline, couple/recipient names, font switcher, moodboard paper finishes, foil ink swatches, and gift box packaging).
